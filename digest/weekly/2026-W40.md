# Week 2026-W40

## The week in AI

- **GPT-6 tiers:** OpenAI's lineup is now Astra, Sol, and Luna; GPT-6.1 Sol was
  reported on 2026-09-29.
  ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-gpt-6-sol-and-luna.md))
- **Gemini 4 Argon** (2026-09-30), Google's most capable model, went first only to
  cybersecurity partners. ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-gemini-4-argon.md))
- **Claude lineup:** Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Haiku 4.5. Thinking is always
  on for Fable and Opus, and forced `tool_choice` and prefill are gone.
  ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-claude-models-overview.md))
- **MCP 2026-07-28 spec:** stateless core, MCP Apps, and Tasks; the largest revision
  since launch. ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-mcp-spec-2026-07-28.md))
- **Open weights:** DeepSeek-V4-Pro (1.6T MoE, MIT) and Qwen3.5 (hybrid linear
  attention, Apache 2.0) lead the open field.
  ([DeepSeek](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-deepseek-v4-pro-model-card.md),
  [Qwen](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-qwen3-5-397b-model-card.md))
- **Agent frameworks converge:** Google ADK 2.11, OpenAI Agents SDK 0.23, and Pydantic
  AI 2.54 (all 2026-10-02) add sandboxes, tool approval, and newer MCP SDKs.
  ([ADK](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-google-adk-python-v2-11.md),
  [Agents SDK](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-openai-agents-sdk-v0-23.md),
  [Pydantic AI](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-04-pydantic-ai-v2-54.md))
