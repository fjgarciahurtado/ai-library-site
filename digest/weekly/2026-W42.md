# Week 2026-W42

## The week in AI

- **Gemini 4 Argon goes public and GA** (2026-10-13): Google's most capable model is on
  the paid Gemini API at $2 / $10 per 1M tokens, doubling after 2026-11-30. It has a 1M
  shared input-output limit and implicit caching only. Its safety report says it solved
  all 12 simulated cyberattacks, so Google applied its strongest safeguards. Penalising
  chain-of-thought made shortcuts silent rather than removing them.
  ([launch](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-14-gemini-4-argon-public-launch.md),
  [safety](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-14-gemini-4-argon-safety.md),
  [model page](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-15-gemini-4-argon-model-page.md))
- **METR embedded at Anthropic:** six METR staff get employee-like internal access and
  can publish without approval, under a new three-tier scheme for evaluator access.
  ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-16-anthropic-independent-evaluators-update.md))
- **Capabilities can be provably hidden:** a paper hides capabilities in model weights
  behind a cryptographic trigger that every tested elicitation method missed, which
  limits what capability evals can prove.
  ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-13-ai-models-hide-capabilities.md))
- **Claude Code moves to stateless MCP:** from 2.1.292 local servers use the 2026-07-28
  protocol by default, and from 2.1.299 remote connectors do too. 2.1.299 also compacts
  idle sessions before the prompt cache expires, and hooks can now fail closed.
  ([2.1.292–296](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-12-claude-code-2-1-292-296.md),
  [2.1.299–300](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-15-claude-code-2-1-299-300.md))
- **Anthropic replaced git:** with over 30,000 agent-driven merges a day, it built Scion,
  a server-side source control system.
  ([source](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-14-anthropic-scion-source-control.md))
- **More models without open weights, and some with:** Meta's Muse Wonder agent models
  are API-only. Mistral's Timestral time-series models ship open weights under a Modified
  MIT licence.
  ([Muse Wonder](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-13-meta-muse-wonder.md),
  [Timestral](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-16-mistral-timestral-time-series-models.md))
