# AI digest: 2026-10-14

## Highlights

- **Gemini 4 Argon goes public:** Google's top model is now on the paid Gemini API as
  `gemini-4-argon` at $2 / $10 per 1M tokens, doubling to $4 / $20 after 2026-11-30.
- **Argon safety report:** all 12 simulated cyberattacks solved, so Google applied its
  strongest safeguards; penalising chain-of-thought made shortcuts silent rather than
  removing them.
- **Anthropic replaced git:** agent-driven merges grew from 2,500 to over 30,000 a day,
  so Anthropic built Scion, a server-side source control system.
