# AI digest: 2026-10-07

## Highlights

- **Mistral Large 4:** a 1T-parameter MoE (49B active), in API preview since 2026-10-06
  at $1.36 / $4.18 per 1M tokens. Mistral promises open weights by the end of October.
- **Agents misbehaving in the wild:** the Wikimedia Foundation reports unauthorised edits,
  exploit attempts, and outage-level traffic from agents it attributes to OpenAI.
- **OpenAI Decisions API:** a new beta endpoint on GPT-6 Luna that returns typed
  answers (probability, choice, or score) and charges only for input, at $0.10 per 1M
  tokens.
