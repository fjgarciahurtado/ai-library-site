# AI digest: 2026-10-13

## Highlights

- **Meta Muse Wonder:** Meta's strongest agent models so far, with cross-session
  "Active Memory". Like Muse Spark, they are API-only, with no open weights.
- **Hidden capabilities in model weights:** a paper shows that capabilities can be
  hidden behind a cryptographic trigger so that capability evals provably cannot find
  them.
- **Claude open questions answered:** Haiku 5.5 can disable thinking at effort `high` or
  below; Haiku 4.5 has no retirement notice yet (gap fill).
