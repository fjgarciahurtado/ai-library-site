# AI digest: 2026-10-08

## Highlights

- **Claude Haiku 5.5:** released 2026-10-07 with 1M context, effort control, and computer
  use, at $0.10 / $0.50 per 1M tokens for prompts up to 100K tokens (5x above that).
- **Cheaper Claude cache reads:** Sonnet 5.5 cache reads now cost $0.10 per 1M tokens,
  5% of input, matching Opus 5.5's discount.
- **EmbeddingGemma 2:** Google's open (Apache 2.0) 740M-parameter embedding model covers
  text, code, images, audio, and video, and runs on a phone.
