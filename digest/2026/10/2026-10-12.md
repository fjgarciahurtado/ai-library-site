# AI digest: 2026-10-12

## Highlights

- **Claude Code defaults to the stateless MCP protocol:** since 2.1.292 (2026-10-06),
  local MCP servers are connected with the 2026-07-28 spec by default, the first major
  client in the library to do so. Hooks can now fail closed.
- **Gemini 3.7 Flash and 3.5 Flash deprecated:** requests are silently routed to 3.8 Flash
  and 3.6 Flash; the December 2025 Deep Research preview shuts down on 2026-10-23.
