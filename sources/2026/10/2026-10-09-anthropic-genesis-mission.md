## Summary

Anthropic committed $150 million over three years to the Genesis Mission, the US federal
initiative for AI-driven scientific discovery. The announcement was made at the White
House Office of Science and Technology Policy's "Science: A New Golden Age" summit.

## Key points

- Claude will be available to more than 15 federal agencies, including NASA, NIH, and
  NSF.
- Claude, Claude Code, and API credits go to "several hundred" Genesis Mission research
  projects over three years.
- Planned joint work with agencies on priorities such as fusion energy and quantum
  computing, plus training and technical support for scientists.

## Why it matters

Shows frontier labs competing for government science work with credits and staff, not
only model access. Claude Code is part of the offer, which suggests coding agents are now
seen as research tools.
