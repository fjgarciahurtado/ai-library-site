## Summary

Shows that prompting large models with worked examples that include intermediate
reasoning steps greatly improves their performance on arithmetic, commonsense, and
symbolic reasoning tasks.

## Key points

- The effect appears only at sufficient model scale.
- A few step-by-step examples in the prompt are enough.
- Gains are largest on multi-step problems.

## Why it matters

The starting point for "thinking" models, which now produce such reasoning internally
and are trained to use it.
