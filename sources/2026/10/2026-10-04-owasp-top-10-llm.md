## Summary

A community-maintained list of the most critical security risks for applications built
on large language models, with descriptions, examples, and mitigations.

## Key points

- Prompt injection is ranked as the top risk.
- Other entries include sensitive information disclosure, supply chain risks, data and
  model poisoning, improper output handling, excessive agency, and unbounded consumption.
- Each risk comes with prevention guidance.

## Why it matters

The standard checklist for threat-modelling an LLM application or agent.
