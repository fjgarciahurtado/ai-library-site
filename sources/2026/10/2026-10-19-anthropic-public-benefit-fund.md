## Summary

Anthropic is setting up the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund and giving it 5% of the
company's shares, subject to stockholder approval. The Fund can grow as the company's
value rises. The equity comes from what would otherwise have been escalating executive
compensation awards. The Fund is meant for giving outside Anthropic's own mission work,
such as large donations to nonprofits or direct payments to affected groups.

## Key points

- Initial stake: 5% of shares. The board may add 2% for every 50% rise in company value
  above a baseline. Anthropic has not published the baseline.
- Legal form: a wholly owned LLC, not a 501(c)(3), chosen for flexibility across types of
  giving. Its Anthropic equity may not fund lobbying or political giving anywhere.
- Mission work stays inside the company: safety and interpretability research, applied
  biomedical research, and Claude discounts for nonprofits and science.
- Example area: labour-market impacts of AI, where Anthropic previously committed $200M
  to research and says bolder approaches may be needed.
- With the co-founders' pledge to donate 80% of their equity and an employee donation
  match (about half of staff take part), Anthropic says about 20% of its shares will go
  to the public good, plus any milestone additions.
- Governance and valuation milestones will be described "in the coming months", along
  with a public benefit report.

## Why it matters

A frontier lab is committing a fixed share of its equity, not a cash budget, to
charitable uses, so the amount grows with the company's success. How the Fund will be
governed is not yet public.
