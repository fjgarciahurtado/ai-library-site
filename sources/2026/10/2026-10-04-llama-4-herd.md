## Summary

Meta's launch post for Llama 4, its first mixture-of-experts and natively multimodal
Llama models. It released two open-weight models, Scout and Maverick, and previewed
Behemoth, a much larger teacher model that was still in training.

## Key points

- Scout: 17B active and 109B total parameters, 16 experts, 10M-token context window.
- Maverick: 17B active and 400B total parameters, 128 experts.
- Behemoth (preview, not released then): 288B active, about 2T total, 16 experts.
- Text, image, and video input.
- Weights on llama.com and Hugging Face under Meta's custom Llama 4 licence, which is
  not an OSI open-source licence and adds usage conditions.

## Why it matters

Brought MoE and very long context to the Llama line. As of October 2026 it is the most
recent Llama release recorded in this library.
