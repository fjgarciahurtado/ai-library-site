## Summary

Anthropic opened its fifth Asia-Pacific office, in Singapore, and signed a memorandum of
understanding with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI) on
AI safety, evaluation, adoption research, and skills. The post lists local customers and
notes in-region inference for regulated firms.

## Key points

- MOU scope: cooperation on AI safety, security, and model evaluation; technical
  exchanges with Singapore's AI assurance institutions on testing standards and incident
  response; skills for SMEs and public officers; sharing Anthropic Economic Index data.
- GovTech Singapore has over 1,500 engineers and technical staff using Claude Code.
- Other users named: DBS, GIC, Grab (BriX sales assistant, about 40,000 hours saved per
  quarter), Sea/Shopee, Supabase; the Cyber Security Agency is exploring Claude for cyber
  defence.
- Regulated organisations can run Claude with in-region inference on Amazon Bedrock.
- Singapore ranks 2nd of 121 countries for Claude.ai use relative to population.

## Why it matters

Mostly industry news, but it shows government agreements increasingly covering joint
model evaluation, and in-region inference as the route for regulated customers.
