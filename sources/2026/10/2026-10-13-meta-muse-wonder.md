## Summary

Meta's announcement of Muse Wonder and the larger Muse Wonder Full, which it calls its
most capable models for personal and professional agent tasks. They follow Muse Spark
1.1 (July 2026) and, like it, are offered only through Meta's API and products, not as
open weights. The post names benchmarks but gives no scores, and promises a separate
results report and a safety report.

## Key points

- **Availability (2026-10-12):** Muse Wonder through the Meta Model API and Muse Code;
  Muse Wonder Full only for Muse Code subscribers. It will later power the Muse app,
  starting with subscribers. No prices were given.
- **Active Memory:** carries context about the user across conversations and pulls in
  relevant details for the current task.
- **Active Focus:** keeps working on hard goals by trying other approaches, working
  around blockers, and checking in before important actions.
- Claims state-of-the-art results on agent, memory, and coding benchmarks, naming
  Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark (LAB), FocusBench, and SWE-Marathon. No numbers in the
  post itself.
- Trained at Meta's Prometheus data centre on "larger, stronger base models"; no
  parameter counts. Meta calls it its "most aligned model to date", with stronger CBRN
  and cyber safeguards.
- No mention of open weights or a licence.

## Why it matters

Meta's frontier line remains closed and API-first, and it now competes directly on
long-running agents with built-in cross-session memory. The benchmark claims cannot be
checked until Meta publishes the results report.
