## Summary

On 2026-10-06 OpenAI simplified its API usage tiers from five to three paid tiers:
Build, Launch, and Grow. An organisation moves up automatically when its total credit
purchases reach each tier's minimum. The rate-limits guide gives the thresholds and the
monthly usage limits for each tier.

## Key points

- Free (allowed geographies only): $100 / month usage limit.
- Build: after $5 in total credit purchases; $500 / month.
- Launch: after $100; $5,000 / month.
- Grow: after $500; $200,000 / month.
- Monthly usage limits are separate from the spend limits an organisation configures,
  and from the special rate limits for the `ultrafast` service tier.
- Figures as of 2026-10-07.

## Why it matters

New projects reach high usage limits after a much smaller spend than under the old
five-tier scheme. That lowers the barrier for teams scaling up on the OpenAI API.
