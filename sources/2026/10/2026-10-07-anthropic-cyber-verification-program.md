## Summary

Anthropic merged Project Glasswing and its earlier Cyber Verification Program into one
programme with three access tiers. Verified security professionals get Claude with fewer
cyber-related restrictions for defensive and offensive-testing work. The programme covers
Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Mythos 5.1, and future models.

## Key points

- **Defense Access:** security teams at companies, nonprofits, universities, and
  government; critical infrastructure operators; small security firms; open-source
  maintainers; individual researchers with a record of vulnerability disclosure. Review
  takes a few days.
- **Red Team Access:** in-house and government red teams and professional penetration
  testing firms. Organisations only, not individuals. Review takes a few weeks.
- **Specialized Access:** a small set of verified organisations testing safety-critical
  systems such as flight operations, power grids, and telecoms, reviewed together with
  the US government.
- Anthropic says Glasswing partners found at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities
  between April and July 2026, over 33,000 rated critical or high severity.
- Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, the related controls for enterprise customers, are due
  in autumn 2026.

## Why it matters

Frontier labs now restrict cyber capabilities by default and unlock them for verified
users. Google takes the same approach with Gemini 4 Argon. Access to Claude Mythos 5.1 now
goes through this programme, not through a separate Glasswing partnership.
