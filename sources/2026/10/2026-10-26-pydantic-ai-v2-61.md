## Summary

Pydantic AI 2.61.0 (2026-10-23, from the GitHub release feed) adds structured citations
on text parts. Citations from Bedrock, OpenRouter, and xAI are converted to one shape,
and they are sent back to the provider that produced them on later turns. The release
also adds the Kimi K3.1 Pro models, a log-size limit for background shell jobs, and a
public extension surface for durable execution.

## Key points

- **Citations:** a `TextPart` can carry structured citations in one normalized form,
  whichever of Bedrock, OpenRouter, or xAI produced them. This makes it easier to show
  sources in a RAG or search app without code for each provider.
- **Models:** `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed` were added (see the Kimi
  K3.1 Pro note).
- **Shell jobs:** `Shell.max_log_bytes` limits the logs kept from background and shell
  jobs.
- **Realtime:** `RealtimeClientSecret.webrtc_url` (with `webrtcfilter=on` on Azure
  OpenAI) and `RealtimeModelProfile.supports_audio_output`.
- **Durable execution:** the durable engine's extension surface, including operation
  types and `DurableRunContext`, is now public, so third parties can write engines.
- **Upgrade:** `pip install -U pydantic-ai`.

## Why it matters

Citations in one shape across providers remove a common chore in source-grounded apps.
Pydantic AI supported the new Kimi model on its launch day.
