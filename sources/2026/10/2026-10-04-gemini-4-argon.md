## Summary

Google released Gemini 4 Argon, its most capable model, on 2026-09-30, but only to
selected cybersecurity partners through its Fairwind Program.

## Key points

- Strong at defensive security: can find, validate, and patch software vulnerabilities.
- Also targets coding, codebase migration, visual analysis, and deep reasoning.
- Not generally available at launch.
- The Gemini app passed one billion monthly users in August 2026.

## Why it matters

A frontier model released first to security partners rather than to the public, a sign
of how seriously labs now treat cyber capabilities.
