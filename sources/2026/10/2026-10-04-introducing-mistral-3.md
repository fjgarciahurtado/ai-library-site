## Summary

Mistral AI's announcement of the Mistral 3 family, led by Mistral Large 3, a sparse
mixture-of-experts model, with every model released under Apache 2.0. Details here come
from Mistral's announcement and its Hugging Face card as quoted in search results.

## Key points

- Mistral Large 3: about 675B total and 41B active parameters, quoted as 673B/39B for
  the language model plus a 2.5B vision encoder.
- 256K-token context window.
- Multilingual, including the main European languages, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic.
- Native function calling and JSON output for agent use.
- All Mistral 3 models are licensed under Apache 2.0.

## Why it matters

The most capable fully permissive open-weight model from a European lab, often chosen
when data must stay in EU deployments.
