## Summary

In July 2026 Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.6 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, and
Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, without a new Pro model.

## Key points

- 3.6 Flash: the general "workhorse" model, with better coding and multimodal results and
  up to 17% fewer tokens used than 3.5 Flash.
- 3.5 Flash-Lite: the cheapest model in the family.
- 3.5 Flash Cyber: fine-tuned for finding and fixing security vulnerabilities.

## Why it matters

Shows Google's 2026 focus on fast, cheap Flash models, with Gemini 3.8 Flash reported
as the newest generally available model in September 2026.
