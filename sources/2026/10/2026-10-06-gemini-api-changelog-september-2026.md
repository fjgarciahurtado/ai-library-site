## Summary

Google's Gemini API release notes for September 2026. Gemini 3.8 Flash became generally
available on 2026-09-02, followed by Live, TTS, and music models and an update to the
Antigravity agent. Gemini 2.5 models are now restricted to existing users.

## Key points

- 2026-09-02: Gemini 3.8 Flash GA, described as the most intelligent Flash model, aimed
  at long-horizon software engineering, autonomous agents, and enterprise workflows.
- 2026-09-03: Lyria 3.5 music generation GA, with full-length songs.
- 2026-09-15: Gemini 3.8 Live GA, plus a Live Extended Thinking variant for voice apps
  that need more reasoning.
- 2026-09-17: `antigravity-preview-09-2026` replaces the May agent version. Built-in tool
  parameters are now PascalCase, and file edits use line-range replacements instead of
  full rewrites.
- 2026-09-18: Gemini 2.5 models are limited to existing active users; new projects should
  use 3.5 Flash-Lite or 3.8 Flash.
- 2026-09-22: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS GA, with voice design and
  consent-verified voice replication.

## Why it matters

This is the primary source for the Gemini 3.8 Flash GA date, which earlier notes only
recorded as reported. It also marks the start of Gemini 2.5's retirement.
