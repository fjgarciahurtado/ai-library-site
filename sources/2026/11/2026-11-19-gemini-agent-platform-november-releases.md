## Summary

Google Cloud's release notes for Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI),
read on 2026-11-19, show which Gemini 4 models are available to Google Cloud customers.
They answer an open question on the Gemini page: Carbon and Hydrogen are both GA there.

## Key points

- **2026-11-10:** Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Carbon for cyber are GA.
- **2026-11-11:** Gemini 4 Carbon in CodeMender (preview).
- **2026-11-12:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen is GA.
- **2026-11-16:** a Deferred tier (GA) for autonomous agent scheduling and asynchronous
  calls on the Interactions API; `gemini-4-carbon` is supported on the global endpoint.
  DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 enters public preview.
- **2026-11-09:** Claude Fable 5.2 GA and fast mode for Claude GA on the platform.
- Gemini 4 Neon (2026-11-18 on the Gemini API) is not listed as of 2026-11-17, the
  latest entry.

## Why it matters

Teams that must stay on Google Cloud can use Carbon and Hydrogen today, but not yet Neon.
