## Summary

Google Cloud's model page for Gemini 4 Argon on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform
(formerly Vertex AI) lists the model as GA, released on 2026-10-13, the same day as its
public launch on the Gemini API. It gives the regions and a retirement floor.

## Key points

- Launch stage: GA; release date 2026-10-13; model ID `gemini-4-argon`.
- Locations: `global`, the `us` and `eu` multi-regions, `europe-west2`, and
  `asia-southeast1`.
- Retirement on the platform: "October 13, 2027 or later". The model may stay available
  on the Gemini API after that.
- Computer use is listed as supported; the Cyber Permissive variant
  (`gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive`) does not support it.

## Why it matters

Argon is GA on both of Google's APIs, so enterprise users with EU data residency
needs can use it in the `eu` multi-region or `europe-west2`.
